# Live performance edition

The player's packed, happy singers determine the notes and voices of a short call-and-response performance after each puzzle. Three A/S/D lanes group bass/brass, piano/brushes and guitar/vibes. The food calls its note a beat before the response. Hit windows, perfect judgments, combos and missed notes produce a bounded bonus, awarded only once when serving. Retrying replaces that performance; editing or undoing a layout invalidates it. The player can skip without losing campaign progress. Rush's departure clock stops during the performance, and Escape pauses the concert itself.

The six-stage simulated input test completed all 48 notes and verified the bonuses in the saved results. Existing packing, rotation, rescue, tea-seat, Cozy/Rush and Free Jam checks passed. The actual browser campaign reached the encore with 18/18 puzzle tickets; real timed key presses hit 8/8 notes on the first performance, and a fifth-stage retry hit four consecutive perfect notes before its screenshot was captured. Other performances were skipped to verify that route. The browser console captured no warnings or errors. All four submission screenshots were refreshed from the running game. The new music interface is in conductor.js; the existing original score and artwork remain in their dedicated files.
